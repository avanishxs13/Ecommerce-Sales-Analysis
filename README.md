# 🛒 E-commerce Sales Analysis (Online Retail II)

End-to-end data analysis of **805,549 transactions** from a UK-based online store (Dec 2009 – Dec 2011) using **Python, SQL, and Pandas**.

## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed 2 years of transactional data to uncover revenue trends, top customers, best-selling products, and geographic patterns for business decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Python (Pandas, NumPy) | Data cleaning & transformation |
| SQLite | Business queries & analysis |
| Jupyter/PyCharm | Development environment |
| Git/GitHub | Version control |

## 📊 Key Metrics

| KPI | Value |
|-----|-------|
| Total Revenue | £17,743,429 |
| Total Orders | 36,969 |
| Total Customers | 5,878 |
| Avg Order Value | £479.95 |
| Date Range | Dec 2009 – Dec 2011 |

## 🔍 Business Questions Solved

1. Overall KPIs (revenue, orders, customers, AOV)
2. Top 10 countries by revenue
3. Monthly revenue trend (seasonality)
4. Top 10 customers by revenue
5. Top 10 products by revenue
6. Avg order value by country
7. Repeat vs one-time customers
8. Best selling months

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. UK Market Dominance (83% revenue)
- UK: £14.7M of £17.7M total
- Top non-UK: EIRE, Netherlands, Germany

### 2. Strong Seasonality
- **Nov 2010 & Nov 2011** = peak months (£1.17M each)
- Oct-Nov consistently highest → holiday shopping effect

### 3. VIP Customer Concentration
- Top customer (ID 18102): £608,821 (3.4% of total revenue)
- Top 10 customers: £2.8M+ (16% of revenue)

### 4. Customer Loyalty
- 72% repeat customers (4,255)
- 28% one-time buyers (1,623)

### 5. High-Value Markets
- Netherlands AOV: £2,430 (highest)
- Australia AOV: £1,789
